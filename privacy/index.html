Sleuthbook privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Sleuthbook ("the app") is an independent, unofficial detective notebook, compatible with the puzzles in the Murdoku books. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by the authors or publishers of Murdoku. Murdoku is a registered trademark of its owner.

What data the app uses

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Photos of the board and the clues (camera or gallery)Detecting the board and reading the printed textOnly on your device
Games (boards, clues, notes)So you can continue laterOnly on your device
Preferences (language, Day/Night theme, helpers)Remembering your settingsOnly on your device
Ad counters (games created and solved, last ad)Deciding when an ad is dueOnly on your device
  • The app has no user accounts and no analytics of its own.
  • Photos and games are never sent to any server: text is recognised on the phone itself.
  • We do not sell personal data to anyone. The only data that leaves the phone is what the Google services described below collect (ML Kit, AdMob and Google Play).

Camera permission

It is only used to photograph the board or the clue cards. You can deny it and pick images from the gallery instead.

Third-party services: Google ML Kit

Text reading uses Google's ML Kit Text Recognition, with the model bundled in the app. Images are processed on the device. According to Google, ML Kit may contact its servers to receive updates and send anonymous technical metrics (device and app information, and API usage and performance) to improve the service.

  • Data collected by ML Kit

Ads: Google AdMob

The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads at only two moments: when you ask for a hint (you choose whether to watch the ad) and when you finish some games. Sample cases and your first 3 games have no ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect the phone's advertising ID (or the app set ID), the IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), device and app information, and interactions with the ads. Google may use them to personalise ads if you give your consent.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for permission with Google's consent message before any ad is shown. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy. If you do not consent, you may see non-personalised ads or none at all, and the app works the same: hints are given even when there is no ad.
  • Advertising ID: you can delete or reset it in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google privacy policy

"Remove ads" purchase: Google Play

You can remove ads forever with a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled by Google Play: the app never sees or stores your payment details. It only asks Google Play whether your account owns the purchase, to remove the ads (also after reinstalling the app or changing phones).

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, the system may include the app's data in your Google backup. This is controlled by your Google account, not by the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete each game from within the app, or all data by uninstalling the app or from Settings → Apps → Sleuthbook → Storage → Clear data.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. The app does not collect personal data on its own; the Google services described above may do so.
